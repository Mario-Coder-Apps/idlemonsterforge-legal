Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the iOS app Colorwall: Cozy Coloring ("the App", "we", "us") handles data. Colorwall is developed by Mario Haesler, an independent developer based in Germany (see Imprint for full contact details). In short: the App collects no personal data. You need no account, and what you color stays on your device.

1. Data We Collect

None. The App has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting service, and it does not send your data to us or to any third party. We do not run a server for the App.

2. Data That Stays on Your Device

Your coloring progress, your finished pictures, your gallery wall, your settings and a record of what you have unlocked are stored locally on your device only. They are deleted when you delete the App. Depending on your device settings, they may be included in your iCloud or computer backups of the device, which Apple handles under its own terms.

3. Purchases

Coloring books and the Colorwall Plus subscription are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your name, your Apple ID, your payment details or your address. To unlock what you bought and to support "Restore purchases", the App receives the transaction information for your purchases from Apple on your device (which product, when it was bought and, for a subscription, when it ends). This information is used only on your device to unlock content. Apple provides us with aggregated sales reports (for example the number of sales per country) that do not identify you. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your App Store account settings. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Information Apple May Share With Developers

If you have chosen, in your device's privacy settings, to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with crash reports and usage statistics about the App. These are anonymous, and we use them only to fix problems and improve the App. You can turn this sharing off at any time in your device settings (Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements).

5. Children

The App is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

6. Your Rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access, correct and delete personal data held about you, among other rights. Because we hold no personal data from the App, there is usually nothing for us to provide or delete; your on-device data is removed by deleting the App. If you have a question, contact us using the details below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Changes to This Policy

If the App changes in a way that affects your data (for example a new feature that needs the internet), we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
