Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the iPhone game Idle Monster Forge ("the App", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices and rights you have. It also covers this website. Idle Monster Forge is developed by Mario Haesler, an independent developer based in Germany (see Imprint for full contact details). We built Idle Monster Forge to be playable without an account, and your progress stays on your device. The data described below is limited to what is needed to process purchases, show optional ads, keep in-game timers fair, keep the game stable and improve it.

1. Data We Collect

1.1 Data That Stays on Your Device

Your save game (monsters, progress, settings, the purchases you own and the identifiers of completed purchases) is stored only on your device, in the App's own storage. It is part of your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer), which Apple manages according to your settings. We have no access to it. On first launch the App creates a random player ID, which you can see in the App's Settings; it is used for the game statistics (section 1.5) and in support e-mails (section 1.8). If you played the earlier version of Idle Monster Forge on the same iPhone, the App took over your progress and player ID from that version's storage on the device once; nothing left the device for this.

Reminder notifications (for example "Raid complete!" or "Your treasury is full!") are planned by the App and delivered by iOS on your device; no server is involved. The App asks for your permission before it shows them, and you can switch them off in Settings › Push Notifications or in your iPhone's settings. To start over, use Settings › Reset Progress (your purchases are kept). To delete everything, delete the App.

1.2 Purchase Data

In-app purchases (voidshard packs, No Ads, the Production Boosts, the Dark Alchemist Passes and the Hero Pass) are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment or account details. The App only receives Apple's signed confirmation of a purchase (the product, an identifier of the transaction, the price and the currency) and keeps the transaction identifier on your device, so that every purchase is credited exactly once. "Restore Purchases" asks Apple for the purchases you own. Product, price and currency are also sent to GameAnalytics as a purchase event (section 1.5). Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

1.3 Advertising Data

Idle Monster Forge offers optional rewarded videos (for example to multiply your offline gold) through Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). When the App starts, it runs Google's consent check (User Messaging Platform, UMP). Where the law requires consent, for example in the EEA and the UK, it shows Google's consent form (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). AdMob starts so that the form can be shown, but the App loads videos only after your choice. From then on the App keeps one video loaded in advance, so that it is ready when you want one. Google processes device information, your IP address and an approximate location derived from it, even if you never watch a video, as well as your interactions with the ads. Google uses the advertising identifier of your device (IDFA) only if you allow it in Apple's tracking prompt. You can change or withdraw your ad consent at any time in Settings › Privacy Options (shown where the consent form applies). Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

1.4 Crash & Diagnostic Data

If the App crashes, hangs or runs into an error, it sends a report to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.; data stored in the EU). A report contains the device model and its technical state at that moment (for example free memory, battery level and screen size), the iOS and App version, your device's language and time zone settings, the error and where in the program it happened, the App's last technical messages, and a random identifier that Sentry creates on your device. Sentry derives an approximate location (country and city) from your IP address; the IP address itself is not stored with the report. The report contains no name, contact details or advertising identifier. We use these reports solely to find and fix bugs.

1.5 Gameplay Analytics

We use GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics ApS, Denmark) to understand how the game is played and to improve its balance and content. The App sends game events: the start and length of a session, tutorial steps, monsters unlocked, Dark Rituals, raids, crafted gear, the Dark Feast, rewarded videos (shown, failed or rewarded, with the place in the game), and purchases (product, price and currency) as well as purchases that Apple takes back. Each event carries your random player ID, a session number, the iOS and App version and the platform (iPhone). The App does not send the advertising identifier (IDFA), the identifier for vendors (IDFV), your device model or your name. GameAnalytics receives your IP address with each request and derives your country from it; we do not receive or store your precise location. We use the statistics only to improve the game, not for advertising.

1.6 Fair In-Game Timers

Offline gold, raids and daily rewards depend on the time. So that changing the device's clock brings no advantage, the App reads the current time from a GameAnalytics server when it starts and when you return to it: it sends a short request and only reads the date and time of the answer. This request contains no player ID and no game data; like every connection, it shows the server your IP address. The App keeps only the difference between your device's clock and the server's time, on your device.

1.7 Ratings

The App may show Apple's rating dialog. You rate directly with Apple; we only see the ratings that are published in the App Store.

1.8 E-mails to Us

If you write to us, for example through Settings › Contact Support, we use your e-mail address and your message to answer you. The e-mail draft from the App already contains your player ID and technical details (App version, platform), so that we can help you; you can remove them before you send it. We delete your e-mails once your request is settled, unless the law requires us to keep them.

1.9 This Website

These pages are served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., a Microsoft company). When you open them, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address in server logs to deliver and protect the site. Where data is transferred to the USA, GitHub states that it relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from third parties.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To deliver and restore your purchases.
  • To show optional rewarded videos and give you their rewards.
  • To keep in-game timers fair.
  • To diagnose crashes and fix bugs.
  • To understand engagement and improve game balance and content over time.
  • To answer your e-mails.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for purposes unrelated to running and improving Idle Monster Forge.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (EEA/UK/GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Consent for personalized advertising and for access to your advertising identifier after Apple's tracking prompt (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time in Settings › Privacy Options or in your iPhone's settings.
  • Contract performance for delivering and restoring the purchases you make (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Legitimate interest for crash reports, game statistics, the server time check, this website's server logs and answering your e-mails (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Our interest is a stable, fair and well-balanced game.

5. Data Retention

Your save game and settings stay on your device until you reset your progress or delete the App. Sentry and GameAnalytics keep crash reports and game statistics according to their own retention rules. E-mails are deleted once your request is settled, unless the law requires us to keep them.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or export data we hold about you, and to object to or withdraw consent for certain processing. Because Idle Monster Forge has no account, the data at our providers is not linked to your name. The game statistics are linked only to the random player ID shown in the App's Settings. To exercise your rights for them, write to us at mariohaesler@protonmail.com with that player ID. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

6.1 Canada (PIPEDA)

If you are located in Canada, the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) gives you the right to know why your personal information is collected, to access it, and to challenge its accuracy. Contact us using the details above to exercise these rights, or contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada if you believe your rights have not been respected.

6.2 Philippines (Data Privacy Act of 2012)

If you are located in the Philippines, the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules give you the right to be informed about how your personal data is processed, to access it, to object to its processing, to have inaccurate data corrected, to have it erased or blocked, to receive a copy of it in a portable format, and to claim damages for unlawful processing. We act as the personal information controller for the data described in this Policy. Contact us using the details above to exercise these rights; if you believe your rights have not been respected, you may file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC) of the Philippines.

6.3 California & other U.S. residents

If applicable state privacy laws (such as the CCPA) apply to you, you may have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context advertising. We do not sell personal data for money. Sharing the advertising identifier with AdMob for ad delivery may be considered "sharing" under some definitions; you can limit this via the App's ad consent settings and your device's ad tracking controls.

7. Children's Privacy

Idle Monster Forge is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. International Data Transfers

Our service providers may process data outside your country of residence, including in the United States and the European Union. Where required, we and our providers rely on appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) for such transfers. If you are in the Philippines, this means your data may be transferred outside the Philippines; we remain responsible for it in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012.

9. Advertising & Tracking Consent

Each time the App starts, it checks with Google whether your consent is required where you are (Google UMP). Where it is and you have not made a choice yet, the App shows Google's consent form before it loads any video. On iOS, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asks you separately before Google may use your advertising identifier. If you decline, you can still watch videos, but the ads will be less personalized. You can review or change these choices at any time in Settings › Privacy Options (where the consent form applies) and in your iPhone's settings (Privacy & Security › Tracking).

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App evolves or as legal requirements change. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective date" above; continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
